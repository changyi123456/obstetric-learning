The original reference organ set uses Visible Human Female data provided by the National Library of Medicine.

Modifications in this project: extracted eight outer pelvic bone meshes (bilateral ilium, ischium and pubis, sacrum, coccyx); removed other body structures; normalized position and uniform scale; merged coincident vertices; recalculated smooth normals; replaced material; exported GLB. No nonuniform reshaping of the female pelvis.
The adapted object is illustrative and has not received the user's final medical content approval.

The fetal body was authored in Blender for this project. It is a simplified educational illustration, not a biometric or biomechanical simulation.
